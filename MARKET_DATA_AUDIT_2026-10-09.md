# Athens Markets — source audit (09 October 2026)

## Scope and source rules

- The public Leaflet/Folium map has 27 Bio markers and 18 pre-existing regular-market markers.
- Each Bio operating time below was checked on the **individual market page** of the [Attica Organic Producers' Association](https://www.bioagores.org/), not extrapolated by weekday.
- Map coordinates for 25 active Bio markets were read from Google Maps embeds published on each respective Bioagores market page. These are **map centres**, not surveyed market boundaries; the written street location prevails.
- Elliniko and Glyfada remain as historically listed, *temporarily closed*; no hours were fabricated.
- The 18 original regular-market pins are **legacy, not a complete or fully reverified Attica register**. See [current official ordinary market register](https://www.foreaslaikon.gov.gr/markets-map/) for current street rotations. Official [general sale hours](https://www.foreaslaikon.gov.gr/faq/) are 07:30–15:30, exceptional circumstances excepted.
- For every day selected in the on-map controls, a link points to the official regular-market register filtered to that weekday.
- Coordinates, source descriptions and statuses were checked against publisher pages on **09 October 2026**; this static map **does not automatically synchronize**.
